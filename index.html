📋 Purpose of Data Collection

The Guidance Services Office of Manuel V. Gallego Foundation Colleges (MVGFC) collects your personal information and survey responses for the following purposes:

  • To evaluate and improve the quality of guidance services
  • For institutional research and program development
  • To monitor student welfare and satisfaction
  • For academic and administrative reporting

🔐 How Your Data Is Protected

Your responses are stored securely and accessed only by authorized Guidance Office personnel, in accordance with:

  • Republic Act 10173 – Data Privacy Act of 2012
  • National Privacy Commission (NPC) guidelines
  • MVGFC's internal data protection policies

📊 Use for Research & Study

Aggregated and anonymized data may be used for research and institutional reports. Your individual identity will never be disclosed in any published output. You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data by contacting the Guidance Office.

For questions about your data, contact the MVGFC Guidance Services Office.
